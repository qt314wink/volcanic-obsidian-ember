# 🌋 Volcanic Obsidian Ember

> *Obsidian goddesses, magma kintsugi, cinder blooms — a mythpunk descent into the inferno.*

An interactive generative canvas experience built around volcanic aesthetics: ember particles rising from the bottom of the screen, magma vein paths that trace across obsidian backgrounds, SVG kintsugi cracks in red-gold gradients, and click-triggered fire-flower bursts with rumble audio synthesis.

## What It Does

- **Magma Vein Engine** — 12 autonomous `MagmaVein` agents leaving glowing orange trails that fade and renew organically
- **Ember System** — 80 ambient floating embers with hsla color variation, plus burst-mode embers on click
- **Ash & Smoke** — Falling ash particles and rising radial-gradient smoke columns for atmospheric depth
- **Mouse Trail** — Leaves glowing fire-colored decay particles that fade as you move
- **SVG Kintsugi Overlay** — Static crack paths in magma/gold palette with `drop-shadow` glow filter and pulse animation
- **Web Audio Rumble** — Click triggers layered sawtooth oscillator, noise burst, and sub-bass sine for a percussive volcanic sound
- **GSAP ScrollTrigger** — Section reveals with staggered x/y entrance and ember divider pulse animation
