## Repository-specific records

### D28 — Elemental Mythpunk: separate worlds with an authored narrative grammar

**Use when:** When recovering luminous-lunarpunk, desert-mirage-nomad, arctic-frost-shaman, volcanic-obsidian-ember, or related elemental worlds.

[Recovered intent] The series uses fracture/repair, botanical emergence, elemental atmosphere, and interactive audio. Preserve distinct worlds:
• Luminous Lunarpunk: stained glass, gold repair lines, botanical teal/jade, goddess reassembly.
• Desert Mirage Nomad: terracotta/ochre dunes, copper fractures, oasis bloom, wind/sand.
• Volcanic Obsidian Ember: dark obsidian, magma trails, ash/smoke, fire flowers, percussive rumble.
• Arctic Frost Shaman: pale blue/silver/glacial white is documented, but its README says “Likely Contains”; do not promote those inferred features to implementation.

[Stack] The retrieved descriptions center on Canvas/SVG, HTML/CSS, Web Audio, and GSAP. These are authored visual/narrative pieces, not scientific environmental simulations.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Define a common scene/interaction shell only where it preserves each world's story. Separate scene identity, palette, structural motifs, particle field, narrative beats, input, audio, and accessibility. Record cultural references accurately; a broad “Berber”/“shamanic” label is insufficient provenance for a specific motif or tradition.

[Reimagined horizon] A connected anthology of repair/renewal experiences, with authored worlds, standalone art pages, reusable atmospheric mechanisms, and possible print/fashion/installation derivatives.

[Carry-forward test] Each recovered world has a coherent beginning, participatory action, visible transformation, meaningful ending, and static/muted/keyboard path. The shared shell must not reduce all worlds to palette-swapped particles.

[Unresolved siblings] solar-cyberpunk-botanist and neon-gothic-kintsugi appeared in repository inventory but README retrieval failed. Preserve them as recovery targets, without inventing their original stories.

## Practical continuation contract

1. Re-read current repository instructions and source before implementation. The baseline above identifies this publication, not a permanent execution HEAD.
2. Retain the full design horizon separately from the current demonstrator. Name what the demonstrator preserves, approximates, defers, or cannot prove.
3. Map tokens and controls to actual consumers with explicit units, ownership, accessible alternatives, and observable effect.
4. Complete the relevant interaction/error/cancellation states before promoting a reusable component.
5. Pair source/runtime checks with human correspondence review against the actual visual inspiration.
6. Keep proposed repairs unexecuted until a later implementation records its own revision, checks, and evidence.
